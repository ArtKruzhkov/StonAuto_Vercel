About Us

Welcome to Ston Auto Restyling, where automotive passion meets precision craftsmanship.

Premium Automotive Protection & Restyling

STON Auto Restyling is your destination for premium automotive protection and bespoke vehicle enhancement in Santa Monica. Founded in 2024, we are driven by a clear vision — to redefine the standard of luxury automotive care through precision, innovation, and uncompromising attention to detail.

We don’t just modify vehicles — we curate automotive experiences. Every project is approached with a balance of technical expertise and refined aesthetic vision, ensuring results that are both visually striking and built to last.

Our team focuses on precision installation techniques, premium material sourcing, and concours-level detailing to deliver finishes that exceed expectations. From protective solutions to full restyling transformations, every vehicle that enters our space leaves elevated to its highest potential.

At STON Auto, craftsmanship is not a service — it’s our standard.

Driven by Quality. Defined by Standards.

At STON Auto Restyling, quality is not an upgrade — it’s the baseline. Every vehicle that enters our space is treated with uncompromising attention, discipline, and respect for the craft.

We operate with a process-driven approach, ensuring consistency, precision, and flawless execution at every stage. From initial preparation to final delivery, each detail is carefully refined to meet the expectations of clients who value excellence.

Our reputation is built on trust, consistency, and results that speak for themselves — because true quality is seen, felt, and experienced in every finished vehicle.
